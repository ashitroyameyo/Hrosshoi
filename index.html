About

Art is
resistance.

Hrossho i began as a living room for people who make things and people who love what they make. It has grown into a home for gigs, supper clubs, readings and healing circles, with a second home in Jamaica, Queens, New York.

Is it free to come?

Many nights are free. Some, like the Table, have a ticket price which is listed on the event page.

Where exactly is it?

Nikunja, Dhaka. We send the full address when you RSVP.

Can I host my own event?

Yes. Artists, collectives and anyone with something to share can enquire. Tell us the idea, the date you have in mind and roughly how many people.

Do I need to speak Bangla?

No. The programme moves between Bangla and English, and everyone is welcome.
